# 2026 Home Office Buying Brief

Generated example for ViewMD launch screenshots.

## Executive Summary

Remote workers do not need the most expensive desk setup. They need a setup that reduces daily friction: clear audio, stable video, comfortable posture, and a desk surface that does not fight the work.

The best default package for 2026 is a midrange standing desk, an external 4K display, a dedicated microphone, and a task chair with adjustable lumbar support.

## Recommendation

Choose the "Focused Professional" setup if the user spends 25 or more hours per week at the desk.

| Category | Recommended Choice | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Desk | Electric standing desk, 48 to 60 inches | Prevents posture lock-in and supports focused work blocks |
| Chair | Adjustable ergonomic task chair | Reduces back and shoulder strain during long sessions |
| Display | 27-inch 4K monitor | Improves reading, review, and multi-window work |
| Audio | USB microphone or quality headset | Makes meetings and recordings easier to understand |
| Lighting | Soft key light plus indirect room light | Reduces harsh shadows and eye fatigue |

## Buying Priorities

1. Spend first on the chair and display.
2. Avoid decorative accessories until the basics are stable.
3. Pick equipment that can be adjusted without tools.
4. Keep cable management simple enough to maintain.

## Risk Notes

- Very wide desks invite clutter if the user does not already have a filing habit.
- Cheap monitor arms often wobble during typing.
- Ring lights can cause glare on glasses.
- Premium chairs are not automatically better if the fit is wrong.

## 30-Minute Setup Checklist

- [ ] Place the monitor at eye height.
- [ ] Set chair height so feet rest flat.
- [ ] Put the microphone within 8 to 12 inches during calls.
- [ ] Route power and display cables behind the desk.
- [ ] Test one video call before the next meeting.

## Final Takeaway

The best home office is not the one with the most gear. It is the one that makes work feel easier every day.
